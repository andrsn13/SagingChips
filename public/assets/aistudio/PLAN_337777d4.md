## 1. What we're building

A single-page **marketing website** for Saging Chips, a banana chip brand based in Antipolo City, Philippines.

The site is **not an online store.** Its job is to make people want the product and then send them to social media to order.

### Primary goal
**Make people want Saging Chips.** Visitors should notice the product, get interested, discover the flavors, understand what they get, and feel motivated to message us to order.

### Secondary goals
1. Attract and convert prospective **resellers**.
2. Strengthen **brand recognition** and identity.

### The visitor's journey (a working assumption, not a fixed sequence)
1. **Attention**: something catches the eye.
2. **Desire**: "That looks delicious. I want to try it."
3. **Discovery**: "What flavors are there? What makes it interesting?"
4. **Intent**: "I want to buy this."
5. **Handoff**: the visitor goes to Instagram or Facebook to order.

**How we measure success:** how many visitors click through to the ordering channels, not how much they admire the site.
The risk to avoid is a site that looks great but doesn't make anyone want to buy banana chips.

### Why people might want it, and how the site can show it
| Reason | How the site expresses it |
|---|---|
| Sensory appeal (taste, crunch, look) | Close-up food photos, texture, motion |
| Value for money | Price shown on every flavor; 70 g quantity shown clearly |
| Variety | Four flavors to choose from, presented as "find your favorite" |
| Fits the occasion | School, work, road trips, sharing with friends |
| Quality and trust | Credible product presentation, pack details, maker credit, real business info |
| Novelty | Distinctive branding, unexpected layout, motion |

Rule: **don't claim what we can't support.** No "healthier", "premium", "best" or "crispiest" without evidence.

Source: the strategy discussion in [conversations.txt](conversations.txt).

## 4. Brand identity

### 4.1 Logo cleanup
The real logo (Giaza wordmark) **replaces** the mockup's script-in-a-circle logo. The mockup's logo was AI-invented.
The wordmark's character is worth keeping: high-contrast serif, ball ends, looping "g" tails, a warm retro feel that suits the orange "Crunch Cult" direction.
The owner has approved cleaning the logo up so it follows standard design practice.

| Problem | Principle | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "CHIPS" and "By Gian Geverola" are tiny, in a generic sans, placed at arbitrary positions | Hierarchy, alignment | A proper **"BANANA CHIPS"** line in spaced capitals, aligned to the wordmark's edges. **Remove the byline from the logo.** |
| Thin strokes and g loops break up at small sizes | Legibility at small sizes | Three versions: **full lockup**, **wordmark only**, **compact "S" mark** (favicon, Instagram avatar, sticker corner) |
| The g loops nearly touch each other and neighboring letters | Avoid near-touches between curves | Re-space the letters and adjust the loops so curves clearly overlap or clearly separate |
| No spacing or minimum-size rules | Consistent brand use | Clear space ≈ height of the "a"; set a minimum width per version |
| Only exists as a 500px image | Production quality | Clean SVG vectors; one-color versions (white, burnt orange, dark brown) |

**Maker credit:** "Made by Gian Geverola in Antipolo" goes in the About section and footer, where it supports trust.

**Getting the exact letterforms:** Giaza is commercial and not installed here. Options, best first:
1. **Download the logo from Canva as SVG** (if it was set there). This gives exact outlines without needing the font file.
2. The owner provides a licensed Giaza font file.
3. Fallback: trace the 500px PNG (in progress; quality not yet checked).

Also confirm that **the Giaza license allows logo use.** Canva-provided fonts have their own terms.

All text/background pairs must meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for body text). Check this during the build.

### 4.3 Typography
- **Logo:** Giaza, as vector outlines only. The font itself is not loaded on the site.
- **Display and headings** ("CRUNCH CULT", section titles): **Fraunces** (Google Fonts, variable). Its soft, chunky serif sits well beside Giaza.
- **Body, labels, buttons:** **DM Sans** (Google Fonts). Spaced capitals for section labels like "OUR FLAVORS".
- Keep it to two web font families, self-hosted.

### 4.4 Packaging direction (sticker redesign)
- **Problem:** the current sticker is a generic "Banana Chips" label. **Nothing on the bag says Saging.**
- **Plan:** design **one Saging sticker template in four flavor colorways** as SVG.
  - Used in the website's product renders.
  - Doubles as a **print-ready file** for the vinyl sticker printer.
  - Keeps what the site shows and what customers receive the same, which supports trust.
- **Keep the real pouch format:** 12×20 stand-up, zip, metallic or matte color, bottom window. It's real and affordable.
- Content: Saging lockup, flavor name, "Banana Chips", "70 g".
- **Naming:** pick one name and use it everywhere. The sticker says "Sour & Cream", the mockup says "Sour Cream". **Recommend "Sour Cream".**

## 6. Tech stack

| Layer | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Framework | **Astro** (static output) | Single marketing page; fast; built-in image optimization |
| Styling | **Tailwind CSS** + a small custom CSS layer for wavy dividers and gradients | Quick to iterate; consistent design tokens |
| Motion | CSS animations + a small library (Motion One or GSAP) for floating chips and parallax | Attention and appetite appeal; **respect `prefers-reduced-motion`** |
| Icons | Lucide (UI icons), Simple Icons (social) | Free, consistent |
| Images | Astro `<Image>` → AVIF/WebP, responsive sizes, lazy-loaded below the fold | Performance on mobile data |
| Hosting | Vercel, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages (free tier) | Static hosting with a free HTTPS domain |
| Analytics | Privacy-friendly (Cloudflare Web Analytics or Plausible) with **click tracking on every order/social button** | This is the success measure (§1) |
| SEO / sharing | Title, description, **Open Graph image** for link previews on Facebook, Messenger and Instagram | People will share the link on social media |
| Backend | None | Ordering happens on social media |

**Honesty rule:** product images must match what customers actually receive (pouch shape, colors, sticker).

Long term, replace AI lifestyle shots with real photos where possible.

### Image generation: Canva (connected)
- `generate-image` accepts **reference images** (image-to-image), so the real pouch shape can be kept.
- `remove-background` produces transparent cutouts.
- **Everything goes into the owner's Canva account** (uploads library); it's private and not shared.
- **Claude cannot delete Canva uploads.** Keep things tidy by putting all items in a folder named **"Saging Chips – Website"**.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether generated images also create design files, and whether results can be downloaded into the project as files or only viewed in Canva. The trial answers both.
- **Fallback** if Canva can't deliver files or the quality falls short: an image API key from the owner (e.g. OpenAI image, Google Gemini image, or Flux via fal/Replicate); roughly a few dollars of usage.

## 8. Workflow

1. **Trial** (§10): prove the logo, sticker and pouch-render pipeline on one flavor.
2. **Owner review** of the trial; adjust the direction.
3. **Brand kit:** final logo set, sticker × 4, palette and type tokens.
4. **Imagery:** all pouch renders, hero composite, chips, lifestyle shots. Each image checked visually before use.
5. **Site build:** Astro scaffold → sections in order (§5) → responsive pass → motion pass.
6. **Quality pass:** accessibility (contrast, alt text, keyboard focus, reduced motion), performance (Lighthouse on mobile), link testing (IG DM, Messenger).
7. **Deploy** to a preview URL → owner review → production.
